# Sprint contract D1: SiteDescriptor chain and publisher identity

Status: drafted, pending evaluator challenge (privacy/crypto claim
affected). Spec: `docs/SITE_IDENTITY.md`.

## Requirement being solved

D-01, D-02, D-03, D-04, D-05, D-06, D-07, D-08, D-11, D-12. (D-09 browser
states and D-10 resolution-traffic evidence follow in D2, which touches
Nomad-browser.)

## Files/components expected to change

- nomad-protocol: `docs/SITE_IDENTITY.md` (spec, already drafted).
- nomad-local-reconstruction: new `site` package — canonical binary
  encoding, SiteID derivation, SiteDescriptor encode/verify, chain store
  with rollback and equivocation handling, SitePublication verification,
  identity state resolution, published vectors. The existing 228-byte
  manifest and its domains are not modified.

## Exact threat being mitigated

- A stolen online publishing key used to impersonate a site (recovery
  authority is separate and offline).
- A superseded descriptor replayed to reinstate a revoked key (rollback).
- A publisher equivocating to show different key histories to different
  clients (split view).
- A publication record transplanted between objects, sites or descriptors.
- A malformed or ambiguous descriptor parsed differently by two
  implementations, yielding different SiteIDs for equivalent input.
- An identity failure being presented to the user as mere absence of
  identity.

## Expected positive behavior

- SiteID re-derives byte-exactly from a genesis descriptor; published
  vectors reproduce derivation, digests and authorization messages.
- Rotation authorized by the previous signing majority verifies; recovery
  authorized by the recovery threshold verifies and revokes the prior
  signing set.
- A publication signed by a key active in the highest accepted descriptor
  resolves to `PUBLISHER_VERIFIED` for the intended SiteID.

## Expected negative behavior (all fail closed)

- Rotation signed by recovery keys only, or by a non-majority, rejected.
- Recovery signed by signing keys, or below threshold, rejected.
- Genesis missing a self-signature for any listed key rejected.
- Sequence skip, wrong previous digest, wrong site_id rejected.
- Lower or equal sequence with a different digest rejected (rollback).
- Two distinct valid descriptors at one sequence: site marked
  EQUIVOCATING, transferable proof emitted, no other site affected.
- Revoked key reappearing in a later descriptor rejected.
- Expired descriptor authorizes nothing.
- Publication transplanted to another object, manifest, site or descriptor
  rejected; unknown descriptor yields `PUBLISHER_UNKNOWN`, failed
  verification yields `PUBLISHER_INVALID` (never `UNKNOWN`).
- Malformed/oversized/trailing-data descriptors rejected before any
  signature work.
- Parser differential: JSON with duplicate keys, unknown fields, non-strict
  base64, non-canonical hex or non-UTC times rejected rather than
  normalized.

## Tests/evidence needed to call it verified

- Unit tests for every negative case above, race-enabled.
- Published vectors committed and CI-checked, including SiteID derivation.
- Parser differential tests over mutated encodings.
- Property test: sequence monotonicity and revocation are absorbing (a
  revoked key never becomes valid again along any accepted chain).
- Evaluator review of the diff.

## Explicit non-claims

- No browser integration yet (D-09) and no capture evidence that
  resolution creates no query-dependent traffic (D-10); both are D2.
- No airlock integration; publication records are verified, not yet
  produced through the deposit path.
- Out-of-band SiteID distribution remains outside the claim.
